What can you build?

Enter your words, choose how they are prompted, and build them piece by piece.

Use hyphens to choose the pieces: sh-i-p makes three tiles and un-happy makes two. A word with no hyphens is split into single letters. To add a clue, follow the word with = and the clue, such as re-play = play again. Up to 200 words.

“Hear the word” uses a voice built into this device when there is one; otherwise the browser may use an online voice. A word with nothing else to prompt it is always shown, so students are never left guessing. Extra tiles come from the other words in your list.

Tiles

Tap a tile to add it to the end of your word, or drag it into place. Tap a tile in your word to take it back out.
