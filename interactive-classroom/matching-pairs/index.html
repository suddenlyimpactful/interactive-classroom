What goes together?

Enter your own pairs, then match them side by side or as a memory game.

Separate the two halves with | or =, such as big | large or 3 + 4 = 7. Between 2 and 100 pairs, with up to 80 characters in each half.

Longer lists are split into rounds. Halves that read the same are interchangeable, so two words can share one answer.
