Make time for learning.

Build the clock’s labels, or move its hands to match a time.

With text labels, the top minute position uses “o’clock”; digit labels use “:00”. At 15, 30, and 45 minutes, optional phrases join the number words.

Intervals start at the top: 12 for hours, 00 for minutes. Hour spots sit inside the clock; minute spots sit outside. With every-minute labels, use zoom and the selected-spot panel to keep the clock readable.

This is a 12-hour clock. The hour hand moves between numbers as minutes pass. Move the minute hand through 12 to carry into the next hour. Questions may repeat.
